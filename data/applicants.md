## Cross-cutting watch items

- **Federal Reserve May 2026 pause** on master-account access for uninsured national trust
  banks (cited by Wise as making its original model non-viable).
- Organized opposition: **ICBA** (urging OCC to pause all NTB approvals), **BPI**, **NCRC** —
  all challenging OCC's statutory authority to charter uninsured, non-fiduciary crypto trust
  banks outside the Bank Holding Company Act framework.
- **GENIUS Act** stablecoin rulemaking milestones.
- The four **APPROX.** receipt dates to upgrade via OCC CAS: Fidelity, Crypto.com/Foris DAX,
  Bridge, Laser Digital.
