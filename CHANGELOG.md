- **New entrant: Dakota / Dakota National Trust Bank.** De novo application received by the OCC **2026-07-28** (EXACT, OCC Digital Assets Licensing Applications index). - **Roster-completeness correction: National Digital Trust Company (Seattle, WA; sponsor Protego Holdings Corp.)** added to the Approved & Conditionally Approved table. This is a Feb 13, 2026 OCC preliminary conditional approval (**CD #1366**) that predates this tracker's baseline and had not previously been captured — found this pass while cross-checking adjacent Corporate Decision numbers. Four core business lines: custody, trading, lending/borrowing, and issuer-services (tokenization) platforms, plus fiduciary staking-as-a-service/governance/treasury-management add-ons. Conditions: min $15M Tier 1 capital (≥50%, or $7.5M, in Eligible Liquid Assets), 180-day operating-expense reserve, GENIUS Act conform/cease/divest condition, 12-mo capital-raise/18-mo open expiry. Receipt date is APPROX (control-number prefix implies 2025; not on the current OCC digital-assets index). Six comments were filed on the application (5 bank trade groups + 1 tech-industry coalition per the decision letter); a BPI comment letter dated Oct 14, 2025 is confirmed as one of them. [OCC Corporate Decision #1366](https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-decisions/2026/cd1366.pdf) · [BPI comment letter](https://bpi.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BPI-comment-letter-re-OCC-NTB-application-re-National-Digital-Trust-Company-10-14-25.pdf)

Fed Gov. Waller has targeted a final "skinny" master-account rule by end of 2026.
