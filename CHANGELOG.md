# Changelog

## 2026-08-25

- **World Liberty Financial moves Pending → Conditional.** The OCC granted **preliminary conditional approval on 2026-08-14** to **World Liberty Trust Company, N.A.** (proposed Charter #25407, Control No. 2026-Charter-344521), a wholly owned subsidiary of WLTC Holdings LLC with its main office in Bay Harbor Islands, FL. Approved activities: dollar-backed stablecoin issuance/redemption and reserve maintenance in a **nonfiduciary** capacity — the bank will issue **USD1**, assuming that role from **BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A.**, USD1's current exclusive issuer and custodian — plus digital-asset custody **as a fiduciary** and conversion services limited to custody customers' custodied assets. Conditions: min **$20M Tier 1** with the greater of 50% or **$10M in Eligible Liquid Assets**; **180 days** of operating expenses in ELA (no double-counting); GENIUS Act conform/cease/divest; 60-day notice **and OCC written non-objection** before any significant business-plan deviation; OCC no-objection for all senior executive officers and directors (expressly including CCO, BSA Officer, CTO, CISO, Chief Trust Officer); an independent internal-audit manager; 12-month capital-raise / 18-month opening expiry; conditions run three years. The letter records **seven comments from four commenters** — OCC authority, disclosure and comment-period adequacy, stablecoin-as-deposit, CRA, CFIUS, and conflicts of interest involving President Trump and family, Alexander and Zachary Witkoff, and UAE investors in World Liberty Financial, Inc. The OCC held WLF, Inc. is not a party to the application and that Emoluments/CFIUS questions fall outside its review, and noted the approval was made under authority delegated to career staff. **Passivity commitments dated 2026-06-13** were obtained from StringZ Holdings RSC (DE) LLC, DT Marks SC LLC and AMGUS, LLC. Two facts worth flagging: the bank **will not issue, custody, or deal in WLFI tokens**, and it **"does not plan to seek access to a Federal Reserve Master Account."** It had sat pending roughly seven months — the longest wait in the cohort. [OCC CD #1385](https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-decisions/2026/cd1385.pdf) · [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/world-liberty-gets-occ-conditional-charter-approval-trump/828035/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/world-liberty-trump-occ-bank-charter-stablecoin.html)
- **Zerohash moves Pending → Returned — a new status for this tracker.** The OCC **returned** Zerohash National Trust Bank's application on **2026-07-17** as *materially deficient*; it surfaced publicly around **2026-08-12**. This is **not a denial and not a decision on the merits**: under [OCC Bulletin 2026-27](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-27.html) ("Filing Decision Process," 2026-06-17; [NR-OCC-2026-47](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-47.html)) the OCC may hand back a filing that "lacks sufficient information for the OCC to make a determination" *before engaging in any meaningful processing*. There was **no OCC announcement and no Corporate Decision number**. Zerohash calls it "an administrative process that allows us to refile this month," taken "in coordination with the OCC and … not a substantive decision on the merits," and said it would refile by end of August 2026 with a narrower scope. Reported as the **first return of its kind** in this charter wave. Press has speculated the filing named two different executives as Chief Compliance Officer in consecutive bullets; unconfirmed by the OCC. Table 3 is retitled **"Denied & Returned"** and now distinguishes the two dispositions. [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/zerohash-charter-application-returned-in-first-for-occ) · [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/zerohash-occ-return-trust-bank-charter-application/828036/) · [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/12/morgan-stanley-s-infrastructure-partner-zerohash-rebuffed-in-pitch-to-be-u-s-trust-bank)
- **Method note — how the Zerohash return was caught.** Returns are not announced, carry no decision letter, and generate no press release. The only public signal is **removal from the OCC's Digital Assets Licensing Applications index**. Diffing that index against the roster is now the load-bearing check each pass, and has been added to the cross-cutting watch items as a standing procedural risk for every pending applicant.
