## 2026-09-04

- **A cheaper competitor to the NTB charter appeared, and it is the operating subsidiary.** In [Corporate Decision #1388](https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-decisions/2026/cd1388.pdf) (letter dated 2026-07-23, published September 2026) the OCC approved **SoFi Bank, N.A.** to establish an **operating subsidiary** under 12 CFR 5.34(e) that will "engage in the issuance and redemption of payment stablecoins, including payment stablecoins that are branded by Bank and **payment stablecoins issued on behalf of third-party clients**" — white-label included. The regulatory load is a fraction of this cohort's: **one condition** (GENIUS Act conform/cease/divest, enforceable under 12 USC 1818) plus a 12-month deadline to establish the subsidiary. **No capital minimum, no eligible-liquid-asset floor, no 180-day operating-expense reserve, no senior-officer vetting condition, no three-year condition period** — against $15M–$60M Tier 1, ≥50% in ELA, and 180 days of wind-down operating expenses for an NTB stablecoin issuer. Two consequences now tracked: an entity that already holds a full-service national charter reaches stablecoin issuance far more cheaply than any NTB applicant can, and this lands directly on the **white-label NTB models — Bastion and Agora**. Note SoFi's 2022 charter originally barred crypto-asset activities; SoFiUSD launched Dec 2025 and reached retail May 2026, so CD #1388 formalises and extends a live business rather than starting one.
- **OpenReserve Bank, N.A. → Conditional** (related context, not a trust charter). Preliminary conditional approval **2026-09-02**, [CD #1389](https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-decisions/2026/cd1389.pdf), Control No. 2026-Charter-345612, proposed Charter No. 27203. Full-service **insured** national bank, Salt Lake City, no branches: deposits and lending with tokenized capabilities across all deposit products, payments and treasury, digital-asset services, foreign correspondent banking, full-service BaaS. Residency waivers for **all seven** directors. It plans the same wholly-owned stablecoin-subsidiary structure SoFi just got approved — and **CD #1389 states that subsidiary application has not yet been filed.** Some press called this a "full" national charter; it is **preliminary and conditional**, and the roster says so.
- **Revolut Bank US, N.A. → Conditional** (related context).
